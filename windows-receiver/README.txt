========================================================================
PhoneToUSB - Secure Remote USB Flash Drive Management
Version 1.0.0
========================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS USB DRIVE:

1. Keep PhoneToUSB.exe in the root of this USB drive.
2. Insert this USB drive into any Windows 11 / 10 PC.
3. Double-click "PhoneToUSB.exe" to start the receiver.
4. On first launch:
   - Click "Generate Pairing QR Code"
   - Open PhoneToUSB on your Android smartphone
   - Scan the QR code
   - Click "Approve" on your Windows screen
5. Once paired, you can access your USB files from your phone from anywhere in the world!

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
- This USB drive is the ONLY place where your files are stored.
- Files uploaded from your phone are written directly to this USB.
- The hidden ".phonetoUSB" directory contains internal pairing data and application logs.
- Do NOT delete or modify the ".phonetoUSB" folder unless you wish to reset all pairings.

For technical help and documentation, visit:
https://github.com/phonetousb/phonetousb
